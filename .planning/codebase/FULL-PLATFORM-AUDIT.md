# WisdomBI Gap Report — post-forecast/security sweep, 26 Aug 2026

## 1. Executive verdict

**Not much is structurally rotten — but three things are producing wrong money on live client surfaces right now, and one of them is silent, unbounded, and gets worse every month.**

The four already-audited areas held up. Nothing in this sweep contradicts them. What the sweep found is that the audits were pointed at the *forecast* and the *access layer*, while the failures that survived are in the **multi-entity / multi-currency consolidation path** and in **pipeline-liveness state** — the two places where a number can be wrong without anything throwing.

**Dimensions that came back CLEAN (this is the real reassurance):**

- **Fleet data integrity** — a full-fleet sweep of 96 `business_id` columns across all 31 businesses found **zero orphans, zero duplicate keys, zero dual-ID fragmentation, zero impossible values** (FLEET-10). The dual-ID remediation is holding.
- **Secrets, client-bundle leakage** — all 1,736 commits swept for 14 credential shapes; **exactly one** real secret has ever been committed (SECR-01), and **zero** server secrets reach `.next/static` (10 patterns, 0 hits). No Xero, Anthropic, OpenAI, Resend, AWS or GitHub credential has ever been committed.
- **Reliability primitives** — Xero rate-limit handling (429 three-way split, `Retry-After`), sync idempotency (`begin_xero_sync_job` single-flight, 15-min bounded), partial-sync honesty (partials are never reported as success), and the cron house pattern on the three major per-item crons are all **correct**.
- **Cron scheduling** — all 8 crons on cadence, **0 failed runs and 0 dangling start-markers in 7 days**. The starvation class from #386 has not returned.
- **Client-facing presentation** — the public report at `/reports/view/[token]` is the best-behaved money surface in the app (gates on `has_budget`, shows dollars not percentages, states its own as-at date). Coach dashboard resolves both id-spaces correctly. Save paths are real API saves with debounce + `beforeunload` guards — no surviving legacy local-state-only handler.
- **Dependency risk** — 24 vulnerable packages triaged by reachability; only `next` is reachable (SECR-03). `jspdf`, `axios`, and 20 others provably are not.
- **NFM-05 was refuted outright** — the signed-denominator variance bug is real in the API but the only consumer `Math.abs()`es it and takes the sign from a correct field, so the rendered string is byte-identical to correct. Worth a one-line hardening, not a finding.

**The single most important remaining item: FLEET-01 / NFM-02 — HKD/AUD FX rates stopped on 25 May 2026 and nothing refreshes them.** IICT Group's revenue is being reported at **~5.3× reality** (July: $2,097,736 shown vs ~$398,616 actual; ~A$4.2M of phantom revenue across Jun/Jul/Aug) on the `/cfo` dashboard, the consolidated P&L, and the consolidated balance sheet. On the CFO dashboard specifically there is **no warning of any kind** — `fx_context.missing_rates` is computed correctly by `fx.ts` and then dropped by the caller at `src/app/api/cfo/summaries/route.ts:253-258`. IICT is `is_cfo_client = true`. The dashboard opens on July by default. There is no cron, no scraper, no invariant — the error grows by one month every month, forever, until someone manually clicks a button they have no reason to know exists. This is precisely the failure mode `fx.ts`'s own module doc says it was written to prevent.

The honest summary: **the platform's arithmetic is sound for the 16 single-entity, single-currency businesses. It is not sound for the two multi-entity ones, and it is not sound about whether its own data is current.**
